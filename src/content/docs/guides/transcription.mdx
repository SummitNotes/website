Summit uses a speech model to turn audio into text. A separate AI model handles optional cleanup, summaries, insights, and chat. Configure speech recognition in **Settings → Speech Models** and cleanup or summarization models in **Settings → AI Models**.

## Choose a speech engine

Open **Settings → Speech Models → Transcription Engine**:

| Engine | Model management |
| --- | --- |
| **FluidAudio** | Uses NVIDIA Parakeet. Required models download automatically on first use. |
| **Whisper (OpenAI)** | Choose a model, click **Download**, then **Use Model**. The selected final-transcription model shows **Active**. |

![Speech Models settings with engine and language choices](/docs/screenshots/speech-models.webp)

Both engines run speech recognition locally on your Mac. Download the models before you need to work offline. Bigger Whisper models use more disk space and memory and generally take longer to process a recording.

Under **Language Mode**, choose **English Only** for English recordings or **Multiple Languages** for automatic language detection. For FluidAudio, these modes select Parakeet v2 and v3 respectively. For Whisper, the mode filters the model list; download and select a suitable model after changing it.

For an individual note, open its context menu and choose **Document Language**. Select **Auto** or a specific language. Changing this setting reprocesses the note, so check that it is the note you intended to change.

## Live and final transcripts

Enable **Settings → Recording → Real-Time Transcription → Enabled** for a live preview during recording. Live transcription uses FluidAudio, including when Whisper is selected for final transcription. The final pass runs on the saved audio and may produce different text. Turning live transcription off does not turn off audio recording or final transcription.

## Control what happens after recording

Open **Settings → Processing Flow**:

1. **Auto-generate transcription** starts final transcription when a recording stops. If it is off, use **Transcribe** in the note's recording controls.
2. **Automatically cleanup transcription after completion** runs optional AI cleanup. It can remove filler words and false starts; customize **Cleanup Prompt** if needed.
3. **Auto-generate summaries after transcription** starts summarization after the transcript is ready. Use **Reprocess Summary** from the note's context menu when you want to run it manually or regenerate it.

Choose the AI provider and models before using cleanup or summaries. [Local AI](/docs/setup/local-ai) can run these steps on your Mac; a [cloud provider](/docs/setup/cloud-providers) receives the text needed for its requests. Speech recognition remains local regardless of that choice.

**Generate call summary** creates a Markdown summary in **Notes**. On regeneration, Summit replaces the generated block while preserving your typed notes outside it. [Summary templates](/docs/guides/summary-templates) control the focus of the generated results.

## Review the transcript

Open a processed note and review the transcript alongside its audio. Click a transcript block or timestamp to seek to that part of the recording.

Right-click a transcript block to:

- **Edit** recognized text.
- **Re-transcribe This Segment** or **Re-transcribe From Here to End** using the saved audio.
- **Show Original Text** or **Restore Original** when AI cleanup changed it.
- **Split Block…** to separate a transcript block, or **Assign Person to This Block…** to correct its speaker.

Splitting a transcript block changes its text layout. To turn a long recording into separate notes with their own audio, use [Split Recordings](/docs/guides/split-recordings).

## Identify speakers

Turn on **Settings → Processing Flow → Identify speakers** to distinguish voices in the final transcript. Recognition is imperfect, especially with overlapping speech or poor audio. Review names and assignments before relying on who said what.

Click a speaker name in the transcript to identify or correct the person. Use **Assign Person to This Block…** when only one block belongs to someone else.

**Pause before summarizing when new speakers are found** holds automatic summarization so you can name newly identified speakers first. This helps summaries and action items refer to people by name.

## Understand delayed or missing processing

- **Smart processing delay** postpones transcription and summarization when another calendar meeting starts within 15 minutes. Processing resumes when your schedule clears.
- **Skip summarization on low battery** can skip automatic summaries below your chosen threshold. You can still start a summary manually.
- **Trim leading silence** and **Trim trailing silence** remove silence at the ends before transcription.
- Whisper's **Transcription Sensitivity** controls can help when silence produces false words or quiet speech is missed. Change one control at a time and compare the same short recording.

If a step fails, use **Retry** in the note's status message. If the transcript is empty, play the saved audio and check that the intended source was captured. See [Troubleshooting](/docs/guides/troubleshooting) for model, permission, and processing problems.

## Keep audio if you may need it later

**Delete audio files after processing** in Processing Flow permanently removes the original audio after processing completes. It saves space but prevents later playback and re-transcription. Leave it off if you need to verify quotes, repair recognition, or preserve the recording.
