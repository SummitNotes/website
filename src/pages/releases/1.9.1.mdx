<ReleaseLayout version="1.9.1" date="2026-06-23">

**New Features**
- Record your screen alongside audio, with a pinned video player you can follow while reviewing notes.
- Screenshots now appear inline in the transcript with timestamps, and you can open any screenshot directly from a transcript bubble.
- Send action items straight to Apple Reminders, with assignee suggestions pre-filled from the people in your meeting.
- Organize with nested folders: create subfolders, drag folders to reparent them, and manage everything from a dedicated Folders section in the sidebar with inline create and context menus.
- Choose how your document list looks with new view modes: Default, Comfortable, and Compact.
- Redeem offer codes directly inside the app through the App Store.
- Added OpenRouter as a cloud AI provider.
- Document chats now live in the main Chat menu, with streamlined session controls.
- Beta: Live speaker recognition during recording — Summit now identifies known speakers in real time and can match them as they talk.

**Improvements**
- Recording auto-stop is smarter. 
- Call detection lets you ignore specific apps.
- Auto-summarization can now be skipped when your battery is low to save power.
- Calendar events can be assigned a default recording folder, and calendar dates and times are now properly localized.
- Filter markdown auto-export by folder, with export settings moved into their own dedicated tab.
- Summit now warns you if your selected microphone is also your system output device.
- Improved chat answers for follow-up questions and when no relevant content is found.

**Bug Fixes**
- Summit now alerts you when the microphone captures no audio during a recording.
- Storage safety: you're warned when choosing a non-empty storage folder, and moving your storage location no longer pulls in unrelated files.
- Onboarding now requires choosing a storage folder before you can finish.
- Fixed corrupted AI model downloads.
- Summarization errors are now surfaced instead of silently failing.
